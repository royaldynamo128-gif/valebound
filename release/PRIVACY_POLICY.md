# VALEBOUND — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-14_

Valebound Studio ("we", "us") built **VALEBOUND: Aurora Valley** as a free game with
no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and no account system. This policy
explains exactly what that means for your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The game does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data.

Specifically, the game:

- does NOT connect to the internet (the Android build declares no INTERNET permission),
- does NOT include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs,
- does NOT require an account, sign-in, or any personal information,
- does NOT access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or files outside its own folder.

## What is stored on your device

Game progress (save games, settings, achievements) is stored locally in the app's
private storage on your device:

- Save files: `valebound_autosave.json`, `valebound_slot0..2.json`
- Settings: `settings.json`

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also
reset progress at any time from the in-game settings.

## Children's privacy

The game contains no content requiring age restriction and collects no data from
any user, child or adult. It is safe for all ages (PEGI 3 / ESRB E).

## Permissions

The Android build requests **no runtime permissions**.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online features, this policy will be updated and the
Data Safety declaration on Google Play will be revised before release.
