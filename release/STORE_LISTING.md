## Full description

Rin Ashford came home to Aurora Valley for a funeral — and found a missing person instead.

Your grandfather, founder of the last independent courier guild in the valley, has vanished. His delivery round is yours now: seven settlements, three highways, one valley that Meridian Dynamics is quietly buying up piece by piece.

**A LIVING OPEN VALLEY**
• Drive or walk anywhere across ~36 km² of continuous terrain
• Seven settlements: downtown Port Meridian, industrial Foundry Flats, suburban Willowbrook, Millbrook Village — and four biome cities
• **Four climate regions:** cherry-blossom Petal Vale with drifting pink petals, desert Mirage Basin with cacti and dunes, snowy Frostpeak with alpine pines, volcanic Emberfall with ember fall and an observatory under the cone
• Dynamic day/night cycle and changing weather: clear skies, rolling fog, rainstorms with lightning — plus biome ambiance in each region
• Living streets: traffic follows real routes, pedestrians walk the sidewalks, parked cars line the kerbs

**DRIVE — AND WALK — THE VALLEY**
• Get out of any vehicle and explore on foot, anywhere — then get back in
• **17 vehicles** from a trusty sedan to the comet-fast Ion X — cars, vans, trucks, emergency rigs and **two bikes** with visible riders
• Paint shop: 14 factory colors per vehicle, saved per car in your garage
• Arcade physics tuned for touch: responsive steering, speed-sensitive grip, satisfying drifts, bike leans

**ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO**
• A 10-mission story of small-business survival vs. corporate greed
• Six biome side missions — from festival deliveries to the Founders' Relay across all five city halls
• Endless procedural courier jobs and **taxi passenger jobs** — pick up hailing pedestrians and drive them across town
• Stunt rewards: earn credits for air time and big jumps
• Three fresh **daily challenges** every day with a clean-sweep bonus
• Street races with gates and clocks — beat 60 seconds for the record
• 60 hidden Signal Shards and photo vistas to discover
• Level up to 30, unlock perks, buy vehicles, earn 25 achievements

**BUILT FOR MOBILE**
• Full touch controls: virtual joystick, camera swipe, vehicle pedals
• Three graphics tiers with adaptive quality — smooth on mid-range phones
• Saves automatically after every mission. Play offline, anywhere.

No ads. No in-app purchases. Just the valley.
